; Photo materials (README.md "Photo ground materials"): each section is one material, from a Poly Haven texture
; (polyhaven.com, CC0). The zone files beside this one say which of a zone's textures are made of which.
;
;   asset    the Poly Haven id; its 1K diffuse, nor_dx, rough and disp maps are fetched
;   metres   the ground one repeat of the photo shows
;   magnify  drawn this many times its real size
;   detail   strength of the photo's detail
;   shading  how much of the original's own painted shading stays (rock reads better with some)
;   colour   the material's colour in the game's art (0..1): where splitting a two-material tile starts
